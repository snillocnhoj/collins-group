Robert "Zack" Zackery

Partner | Strategic Operations

Robert L. Zackery III, President of Primer Development Group, has nearly thirty years of experience coaching, developing, leading, and training individuals and teams to succeed. Zack is a Senior Fellow at The Center for the Study of the Presidency and Congress, Washington, D.C.. He serves as Treasurer at the BLK OPS Foundation, Vice-President of the Special Operations Memorial Foundation, and holds the role of Adjunct Instructor at Joint Special Operations University. He has delivered consulting services for a Fortune 2 company, other public and private companies, law enforcement agencies, the Department of Defense and the Department of Justice. Zack has also lectured at the U.S. Air Force Academy and Yale University.
